About Me

I am a UX Developer with a strong passion for transforming ideas into meaningful digital experiences. I specialize in understanding user needs and translating them into clear user flows, wireframes, prototypes, and well-structured interfaces — always focused on creating simple, intuitive, and user-centered solutions that solve real problems.

I have a solid academic background in Computer Science from the Faculty of Computers and Information, which gives me a strong foundation in programming fundamentals, data structures, and problem-solving. This helps me design solutions that are not only visually appealing but also technically efficient and scalable.

In addition to UX/UI design, I bring ideas to life through frontend development using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and React — bridging the gap between design and development to ensure every product is both functional and responsive.

I am continuously improving my skills in both design thinking and modern frontend technologies, with the goal of building impactful digital products that deliver real value to users.

Career Objective

To become a professional UX Developer who bridges the gap between design and development — creating impactful, user-centered digital products that solve real-world problems and deliver measurable value.
